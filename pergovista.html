An outdoor room with a roof you control.

The Pergovista Brisa is a fully motorised bioclimatic pergola. Rotate the aluminium roof louvres for sun, dappled shade or full ventilation — then close them flush to keep the rain out. Designed in Portugal, supplied and installed by Glass Garden Rooms.

Product Overview

One integrated architectural form.

Brisa is designed as a fully integrated pergola system, where structural, mechanical and functional elements are combined within a single, clean architectural form.

The adjustable aluminium louvred roof gives precise control over sunlight, shade and ventilation — so your outdoor space responds effortlessly to changing conditions through the day and across the seasons.

Constructed from powder-coated 6063-T6 aluminium, Brisa delivers long-term durability with minimal maintenance, while keeping a refined, contemporary appearance.

Key Features

Engineered to be lived under.

Motorised Louvred Roof

Rotating aluminium blades open up to ~110° for light and airflow, then close flush into a weathertight roof.

Integrated LED & RGB

Warm-white LED in every fifth blade, plus a dimmable RGB perimeter strip with remote colour selection.

Concealed Drainage

An integrated perimeter gutter channels rainwater down through the internal posts — nothing on show.

Smart App Control

Operate the roof, lighting and sensors by remote or the Tuya Smart app, with optional weather automation.

Sliding Glass & Screens

Add frameless sliding glass doors and zip screens to turn the pergola into an enclosed, all-season room.

Built for the Weather

Wind-rated to ~188 km/h, snow loading to 165 kg/m² and an IP67 waterproof motor system.

One roof, three moods

How the louvred roof works.

The blades rotate through the day, so you choose how much sky, sun and shelter you want — no fixed compromise.

Open

Louvres flat and open to the sky — maximum light and free airflow across the space.

Tilted

Angle the blades for cool, dappled shade while the breeze still moves through.

Closed

Blades shut flush into a solid, weathertight roof — stay out through a downpour.
